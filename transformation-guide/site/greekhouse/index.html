Why this guide

Your members aren't tired of each other. They're tired of the tools.

Since 2020, many members have grown wary of commitments that require showing up in person, they're worn out by video calls, and their phone is where their attention lives. Chapters have responded by adding one more app at a time. The result is three problems that no single app fixes.

Fragmentation

Chat in GroupMe, tickets in Eventbrite, dues in PayPal, the roster in a spreadsheet. Nobody sees the whole picture, and every officer handoff loses something.

Answered by: Unification

Friction

Every task takes one more login, one more link, one more tool that wasn't built for the moment. Members give up halfway through.

Answered by: Fit and Flow

Fatigue

Another Zoom call, another notification, another thread of 300 messages. Members mute the chat and quietly drift away.

Answered by: Feel
