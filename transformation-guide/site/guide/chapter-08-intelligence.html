Intelligence

A home that notices, and gets better every season.

Is the space learning what works, and getting better?

01 · What breaks without it

Most groups notice a member drifting only after they've left.

Engagement rarely collapses overnight. It fades: a member skips a meeting, then a month of messages, then dues. By the time anyone notices, the relationship has gone cold.

Meanwhile the group's knowledge (how the galabanquet was run, which sponsor said yes, who mentored whom) lives in the heads of leadersofficers who will hand off next year. Every new leadership teamexec board starts over.

02 · What good looks like

Two sides of the roof: knowledge and insights.

Intelligence has two halves, like the two pitched sides of the roof:

  • KnowledgeWhat the group knows: its history, documents, decisions and how things are done. Searchable, and drawn from your group's own records.
  • InsightsWhat the group is learning: who's engaged, who's drifting, which events work and what to try next.

Vital signs

Every element in this guide ends with one signal to watch. Together they're your organization'sgroup's vital signs: a short scoreboard of how the whole house is doing. Some of these signals, plus two simple measures, map to the elements of community that McMillan and Chavis proposed:

Element of communitySignal to watch
Membership: I belong hereProfile completion and return visits
Influence: I matter hereShare of members who contributed or voted
Fulfillment of needs: I get something hereA simple "got value this month?" pulse
Shared emotional connection: we've been through things togetherMonthly gathering rate

Suggestions, never surprises

Intelligence advises; people decide. An assistant can notice that members of a programa line haven't RSVP'd and draft a three-step plan to bring them in, but nothing sends until a person reviews it and applies it. The assistant is designed to show only what the person asking could already see.

04 · Ask yourself

Questions for your leadership teamexec board.

  1. How would you know a member is drifting before they stop paying dues?
  2. Where does your group's knowledge live when a leaderan officer hands off?
  3. Which of your events worked best last year, and how do you know?
  4. Who reviews your numbers, and how often?
  5. What should an assistant never do without asking first?

05 · One signal to watch

Members brought back.

↺

The share of members flagged as drifting who come back within 30 days, through a message, a meetup, an RSVP or a renewal. It measures whether insight is turning into action.
